LabTrack: Know Your Report Status

A simple SMS and WhatsApp status tracker that tells patients when their lab report is ready, so they stop making wasted trips to the lab counter.

Problem Description

Patients make repeat trips just to ask if their report is ready

In many public hospital outpatient departments, patients who have given a sample must return to the lab counter to ask if the report is ready. Staff are busy, queues build up, and many people make a second trip for nothing. Elderly patients and daily-wage workers lose half a day of pay or miss their appointments.

Our problem title

Delayed and repeated trips for lab report status in public outpatient diagnostics.

Domain

Diagnostics workflow and healthcare access

Who is affected

Outpatients who have given a sample, their caregivers, and lab staff who answer status queries

Why it matters

Wasted trips cost patients time and wages, and take staff time away from testing

Our Solution

Status messages instead of counter queues

When a sample is registered, the patient gets a token ID by SMS or WhatsApp. When the lab marks the report as ready, the patient gets a message saying where and when to collect it. No results, diagnoses, or test names are sent.

  • Sample registered

    Lab staff enter the patient's phone number and token ID.

  • Status sent

    Patient receives “Your sample is in process” by SMS or WhatsApp.

  • Report ready

    Lab marks it ready. Patient gets “Report ready, collect at counter 3.”

  • Collected

    Patient collects the report. The token closes and the phone number is deleted.

Privacy and Safety

Protecting people's information

  • Minimum data

    Only phone number, token ID, and status are stored. No name, diagnosis, or test type is sent.

  • Deletion

    Phone numbers are deleted 7 days after collection. Token records are anonymised after 30 days.

  • Consent

    Patients opt in by replying “YES” to the first message, and can reply “STOP” at any time.

  • No medical claims

    Messages describe report status only. They never state results or what they mean.

  • Human responsibility

    Doctors and lab staff remain responsible for all results. Urgent concerns go to the lab or a doctor.

  • Security

    Data is held on a hospital-approved server with password access and an audit log of status changes.

FAQ

Common questions

Does LabTrack show test results?

No. It only shares whether a report is in process or ready, and where to collect it.

What if a patient has no smartphone?

SMS works on basic phones. WhatsApp is optional, and counter staff can still help patients who prefer not to use messages.

Can patients stop receiving messages?

Yes. Patients can reply STOP at any time and their phone number is removed from the messaging list.

Who is responsible for the medical information?

Doctors and lab staff remain responsible for all results. LabTrack does not interpret or advise on results.
